# Prompts de geração — Nexo 21

Prompts pensados para ferramentas de imagem e ideação visual. O resultado gerado deve ser tratado como **rascunho**, revisado por designer e verificado quanto a similaridade, legibilidade, anatomia, direitos e coerência com `brand/BRAND.md`.

## Bloco-base de direção de arte

Adicionar ao fim dos prompts quando fizer sentido:

> premium editorial wellness, adult femininity, quiet Christian spirituality expressed through light and presence rather than religious symbols, matte natural materials, restrained composition, linen paper, forest green, muted terracotta, sage and clay palette, soft daylight, generous negative space, tactile realism, sophisticated Latin American editorial art direction, no visual clichés, no exaggerated transformation

## 1. Logo

### 1A — Umbral / nexo

> Design an original vector identity concept for **Nexo 21**, a Spanish-language educational reconnection journey for Christian married women. Create an abstract threshold symbol made from two calm curved paths and one vertical axis, subtly encoding “2 + 1” without drawing literal numbers. The mark should suggest presence, passage, dialogue and distinct people sharing a space — never fusion or control. Pair it with a custom, refined monoline uppercase wordmark “NEXO 21”. Premium editorial wellness, mature femininity, quiet spiritual depth, geometric restraint, excellent negative space, flat vector, one or two colors, scalable from favicon to landing-page header.

**Negative prompt:** heart, wedding rings, church, cross, dove, praying hands, crown, butterfly, infinity symbol, couple silhouette, house roof, shield, lock, flame, sparkle logo, floral wreath, script wedding font, beauty salon logo, metallic gradient, bevel, 3D, mockup, stock icon, copied trademark.

### 1B — Monograma N / 21

> Create 12 black-and-white thumbnail sketches for an original **Nexo 21** monogram. Build the letter N from two independent strokes that approach but do not collapse into one; integrate two arcs and one stable axis as a hidden 21. Use optical balance, rounded terminals, editorial sophistication and a calm sense of forward movement. Every sketch must work at 24 px, avoid obvious religious or romantic iconography, and remain distinctive in silhouette. Flat vector, no gradients, no effects, white background.

### 1C — Wordmark

> Draw an original custom wordmark reading **NEXO 21**. Uppercase, wide but not tech-corporate, humanist geometry, monoline construction with slight optical contrast, rounded joins, generous spacing, mature and serene. Give the X a subtle meeting-point gesture and the O a slightly open inner counter; keep 21 integrated at the same baseline. Provide horizontal, stacked and one-color versions. Do not use an existing font unchanged.

### 1D — Avaliação do logo

> Review this Nexo 21 logo concept as a senior Latin American brand designer. Score 0–10 for distinctiveness, fit with premium editorial wellness, adult femininity, subtle spirituality, small-size legibility, Spanish verbal fit and avoidance of heart/ring/church clichés. Identify accidental resemblance to common symbols or industries. Recommend only structural refinements; do not add decorative motifs.

## 2. Lifestyle

### Regras gerais

- Mostrar pessoas adultas; evitar estética juvenil ou “noiva”.
- Não dramatizar sofrimento para vender.
- Não representar um resultado conjugal como garantido.
- Se houver casal, mostrar duas pessoas com agência, espaço e respeito.
- Pedir diversidade real de idades adultas, corpos e tons de pele ao criar uma série.

> Documentary editorial photograph of an adult Latina woman in her late 30s to early 50s sitting near a window in a lived-in, elegant but attainable home, writing one thoughtful sentence in a linen-bound workbook. Her expression is calm and reflective, not sad and not euphoric. Soft morning side light, forest green knit, warm wood, off-white linen, one muted terracotta detail, intimate 50mm lens, natural skin texture, generous negative space for Spanish headline on the left. Premium wellness magazine, quiet spirituality through stillness and light, no visible religious props.

### 2B — Conversa com espaço

Warm late-afternoon daylight, nuanced expressions, real home, muted forest, clay and linen palette, cinematic but believable, horizontal crop with clean negative space, Latin American casting, no conflict stock-photo gestures.

### 2C — Fé sutil

Forest green shadows, muted terracotta pencil, tactile editorial photography, contemplative, no Bible text legible, no cross, no church, no praying hands.

### 2D — Rotina real

Calm agency rather than exhaustion, documentary realism, natural afternoon light, sophisticated wellness editorial color grade, vertical 4:5 composition with upper negative space for copy.

### 2E — Caminho / umbral

Editorial mobile UI, paper background, forest text, terracotta accent used accessibly, large type, generous spacing, realistic screen reflections, no reviews, no star ratings, no fake user counts.

## 4. Prompt para variações controladas

> Generate four art-direction variations of the same Nexo 21 concept while preserving the logo geometry, exact palette family, mature editorial tone and ethical messaging. Variation A: more editorial; B: more tactile wellness; C: more minimal digital; D: more intimate documentary. Do not introduce new symbols, gradients, wedding cues, overt church imagery, fabricated proof or relationship outcomes.

## 5. Parâmetros sugeridos

- **Logo:** vector/flat mode, 1:1 e 4:1, low stylization, seed fixo durante refinamento.
- **Lifestyle:** 4:5 e 16:9, 35–50 mm, luz natural, stylization baixa a média.
- **Mockup:** 4:5 ou 3:2, alta fidelidade material, texto inserido depois em ferramenta de design quando o gerador não respeitar ortografia.
- **Ads:** gerar imagem sem texto quando necessário; compor copy manualmente para garantir espanhol correto e acessibilidade.
- **Entrega:** exportar master em alta resolução, revisar artefatos e nunca usar asset gerado sem checagem de similaridade e licença da ferramenta.
